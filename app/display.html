Use an old phone as a second screen

An Android phone plugged into this PC can show the album art next to your monitor, and listen to your records with its own microphone. Each step checks itself off when it's done.
1
Get the phone ready (one time)
  • Open Settings > About phone and tap Build number 7 times, until it says you're a developer. On Samsung it's under About phone > Software information. It may ask for the phone's PIN.
  • Go to Settings > System > Developer options (on Samsung, at the bottom of Settings). Turn on USB debugging. Leave Stay awake off: Liner Notes keeps the screen on while it's showing, and lets it turn off when your PC sleeps.
  • Optional: in Settings > Lock screen, set Screen lock to None (or Swipe), so when your PC wakes the phone comes straight back to Liner Notes.

Liner Notes can't see the phone's settings, so check this one off yourself by doing step 3.

2
Install the Android connector on this PC

A small free tool from Google (about 7 MB) that lets this PC talk to the phone over USB.

3
Plug the phone in

Use a USB cable that can carry data (some charging-only cables can't). The phone asks "Allow USB debugging?": tick "Always allow from this computer" and tap Allow.

4
Open Liner Notes on the phone

On the phone, open Chrome and go to localhost:8765. Then tap Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen > Install). From now on, open it from that icon: it runs fullscreen.

5
Listen with the phone's microphone (optional)

The phone sits right by the speakers and records music without call-style noise filtering, so your webcam or headset mic can keep its noise cancelling for calls. The first time, tap the phone's screen once when it asks, and allow the microphone.

An iPhone connects over Wi-Fi instead of a cable. It works as the screen; the listening stays on this PC's microphone, because iPhones only allow the microphone on secure web pages.
1
Share Liner Notes on your Wi-Fi

Lets devices on your home network open the screen. Liner Notes restarts for a moment, and Windows may ask whether to allow it on networks: tick Private networks and click Allow.

2
Open it on the iPhone

The iPhone has to be on the same Wi-Fi as this PC. Point the iPhone's camera at the code and tap the link that appears, or type the address into Safari.

3
Make it an app, and keep the screen on

In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from that icon from now on: no Safari bars. To stop the iPhone dimming and locking, set Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock to Never while it's on the charger.

4
Listening

Records are heard through this PC's microphone. If that mic is on a webcam or headset with noise cancelling, turn the noise cancelling off, or it filters the music out. A cheap USB mic just for Liner Notes is the easy fix.

Replace it after

Most diamond styli last 800 to 1,000 hours; basic conical ones nearer 500. Your cartridge maker lists the figure for yours. A worn stylus sounds dull or harsh and wears your records.
