Use an old phone as the screen.

The Android app works on its own: it listens with the phone's mic and shows the cover, lyrics and credits, no PC needed. If you also run Liner Notes on your PC, the phone can show the PC's screen instead. The PC app has this guide built in too, under Settings > Use a phone as a second screen, and it checks each step off as you go.

Android app

  1. Download it on the phone

    On the phone, open this link and download the app.

  2. Install it

    Open the download. If the phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.

  3. Open it and allow the microphone

    Put a record on near the phone. That's it. The app updates itself when a new version is out.

Connect the phone to your PC over USB

Plugged into the PC, the phone shows the PC's screen, can listen to your records with its own microphone, and sleeps and wakes with the PC.

  1. Turn on developer options

    On the phone, open Settings > About phone and tap Build number 7 times, until it says you're a developer. On Samsung it's under About phone > Software information.

  2. Turn on USB debugging

    Go to Settings > System > Developer options (on Samsung, at the bottom of Settings) and turn on USB debugging. Leave Stay awake off: Liner Notes keeps the screen on while it's showing. Optionally, set Screen lock to None so the phone comes straight back to Liner Notes when your PC wakes.

  3. Install the Android connector

    In Liner Notes on the PC, open the phone guide and click Install. It's a small free tool from Google, about 7 MB, that lets the PC talk to the phone over USB.

  4. Plug the phone in

    Use a cable that carries data (some charging-only cables don't). When the phone asks "Allow USB debugging?", tick "Always allow from this computer" and tap Allow.

  5. Open Liner Notes on the phone

    Open the Android app. It switches to the PC's screen by itself within a few seconds, and goes back to working on its own about a minute after you unplug it.

  6. Let the phone listen (optional)

    In the phone guide on the PC, turn on Listen with the phone's microphone. It sits right by the speakers, so it hears records better than a webcam across the room. The first time, tap the phone's screen once and allow the microphone.

Connect the phone to your PC over Wi-Fi

  1. Share Liner Notes on your Wi-Fi

    On the PC, open the phone guide's Wi-Fi / iPhone tab and turn on sharing. If Windows asks, allow Liner Notes on Private networks.

  2. Open the app on the phone

    With the phone on the same Wi-Fi, open the Android app. It finds the PC within a couple of minutes and asks to connect.

  3. Approve it on the PC

    The PC shows a code. Check the phone shows the same one and click Connect. You only do this once.

iPhone, over Wi-Fi

An iPhone works as the screen. The listening stays on the PC's microphone, because iPhones only allow the microphone on secure web pages.

  1. Share Liner Notes on your Wi-Fi

    In the phone guide's iPhone tab, turn on sharing. If Windows asks, allow Liner Notes on Private networks.

  2. Scan the code

    With the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi, point its camera at the code on the PC and tap the link.

  3. Make it an app and keep it on

    In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from that icon from now on. To stop the screen locking, set Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock to Never while it's on the charger.
