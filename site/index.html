Know who's on the record.

Put a record on. Liner Notes hears it and shows the cover, lyrics, credits and more on your phone or desktop.

Download for Windows

Free for Windows 10 and 11.

Drop the needle.

  1. It hears the record

    Through a mic, your phono preamp or an old phone. Shazam names the song.

  2. It follows the side

    Track by track, even songs Shazam doesn't know. It tells you when to flip.

  3. It knows the release

    Album, greatest hits, compilation or 45.

  4. It shows it big

    Cover, song, side and track. Lyrics scroll along.

That old phone in the drawer is the screen.

Plug an Android phone into the PC and it becomes the display by your turntable. Its mic can listen too. An iPhone works over Wi-Fi.

Getting started

  1. Install it

    No admin password needed.

  2. Pick a mic

    Settings > Microphone. A line from your phono preamp works best.

  3. Put a record on

    That's it.
